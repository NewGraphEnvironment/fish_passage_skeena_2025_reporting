Restoring Fish Passage in the Skeena Region - 2025

Appendix - Assessment Data Summary

Fish passage assessment procedures conducted through SERNbc since 2020 are amalgamated here — assessments, habitat confirmations, design, remediation, monitoring, fish sampling, eDNA sampling and drone imagery. Table 5.3 summarizes the procedures by watershed group; Table 5.4 gives the per-site detail.
